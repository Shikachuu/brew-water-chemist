Brew Water Chemist

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 June 2026

Brew Water Chemist does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app runs entirely on your device. There are no servers, accounts, or analytics involved.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • The water recipes you create or edit (names and mineral concentrations).
  • Your app settings, such as the selected mineral kit.

This information never leaves your device. It is not uploaded, backed up to us, or shared with anyone. (It may be included in your device's own iCloud/system backups if you have those enabled — that is controlled entirely by your device settings, not by this app.)

No analytics or tracking

The app does not use any analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services. We do not create usage profiles and we do not track you across apps or websites.

No third parties

The app does not include third-party SDKs that collect data, and it does not send any information to third parties. QR codes for sharing recipes are generated on your device and contain only the recipe data you choose to share.

No accounts

The app does not require you to create an account or provide any contact information.

Deleting your data

All app data is removed when you delete the app from your device.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Questions?

If you have questions about this policy, please open an issue on GitHub.
