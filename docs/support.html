Frequently asked questions

Where are my recipes stored?

All recipes and settings are stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server. Deleting the app removes all of your data.

How does sharing a recipe work?

The app generates a QR code on your device that encodes the recipe. Another person can scan it to open the recipe in Brew Water Chemist. No data passes through any server.

How do I reset or remove my data?

Delete individual recipes from within the app, or remove the app entirely to clear all stored data.
